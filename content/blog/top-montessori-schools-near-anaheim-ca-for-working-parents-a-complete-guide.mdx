Not all Montessori programs are created equal. When evaluating options for a montessori school Anaheim CA area, consider these essential factors:

<strong>Authentic Montessori Credentials:</strong> Ensure teachers hold recognized Montessori certifications from organizations like AMI (Association Montessori Internationale) or AMS (American Montessori Society). These credentials guarantee educators understand and implement genuine Montessori principles.

<strong>Extended Hours and Flexibility:</strong> Working parents need schools that open early and close late. Look for programs offering full-day options, extended care, and flexibility for your work schedule.

<strong>Age-Appropriate Programs:</strong> Quality Montessori schools offer programs from infancy through elementary years, allowing your child to grow within the same educational philosophy and community.

<strong>Safe and Prepared Environment:</strong> The classroom should be thoughtfully organized with age-appropriate Montessori materials, natural lighting, and distinct areas for different activities.
